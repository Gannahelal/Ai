AI Meeting Debrief

Turn any meeting into structured insights — automatically

What it does

AI Meeting Debrief connects directly to Odoo Calendar and automatically creates a debrief record when your meeting ends. Paste a transcript (or upload a recording for Gemini users), and the AI extracts action items, decisions, and a full sentiment analysis in seconds.

Key Features

  • Multiple AI Providers — Google Gemini, OpenAI GPT-4o, and Anthropic Claude
  • Calendar Integration — "Create Debrief" button on every Odoo Calendar event
  • Auto-Draft Creation — A scheduled job creates a draft debrief 5 minutes after your meeting ends
  • Video & Audio Analysis — Upload recordings for Gemini to transcribe and analyze (MP4, MP3, WAV, and more)
  • Action Items → Tasks — Every extracted action item becomes a Project task, assigned to the right person
  • Decision Log — All decisions posted to the meeting chatter for a permanent audit trail
  • Sentiment & HR Alerts — Detects tension or conflict and flags it for HR review
  • Privacy Mode — Toggle per-meeting to exclude sensitive meetings from AI processing
  • OWL Dashboard — Meeting health KPIs: total meetings, pending actions, sentiment breakdown, HR alerts
  • PDF Report — One-click export of the full debrief as a professional PDF
  • Arabic Support — Built-in Arabic (عربي) translation

How it works

  1. Install the module and add your AI API key in Settings → AI Meeting Debrief
  2. When a calendar meeting ends, a draft debrief is created automatically
  3. Open the debrief, paste the transcript (or upload a recording)
  4. Click Analyze with AI — action items, decisions, and sentiment are extracted in seconds
  5. Action items become Project tasks; decisions are logged in the chatter; HR is alerted if tension is detected

Privacy & Security

  • API keys are stored as encrypted Odoo system parameters
  • Video files are automatically deleted from Gemini's servers after analysis
  • Per-meeting Privacy Mode lets you exclude sensitive meetings from AI processing
  • Role-based access: Users manage their own debriefs; Administrators have full access
